# Types Of Network
- Computer networks are classified by their geographical scope, ranging from personal devices to global connectivity.
- They are classified as below types

1.PAN (Personal Area Network)
-
- A PAN is a small network used for personal devices within a short distance.

#### Examples:
- Bluetooth
- Mobile hotspot
- Smartwatch connection
#### Range:
Few meters around 10 meters
#### Diagram:
     Mobile  ~~~  Earbuds
        |
     Laptop ~~~ Smartwatch

2.LAN (Local Area Network)
-
- LAN connects devices within a small area like home, office, or school.

#### Examples:
- Office network
- Home Wi-Fi
- Computer lab
#### Features:
- High speed
- Private network

            PC1
              |
      PC2 -- Switch -- PC3
              |
           Printer

3.MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)
-
- MAN covers a city or large campus area.

#### Examples
- City-wide internet network
- College campus network

#### Features
- Larger than LAN
- Smaller than WAN

           Office A ----- Office B ----- Office C

4.WAN (Wide Area Network)
-
- WAN connects networks across countries or worldwide.

#### Examples
- Internet
- Bank networks
- Global company networks

#### Features
- Large geographical coverage
- Uses public communication links

            India Office ----- USA Office ----- UK Office
